# Ready or Not: SWAT Tactical Shooter and Mission Based Gameplay

---

## What Is Ready or Not?

Ready or Not is a first person tactical shooter developed and published by VOID Interactive. The game places players in the role of a SWAT team leader responding to dangerous incidents across the fictional city of Los Sueños.

Rather than focusing solely on action, Ready or Not emphasizes careful observation, squad coordination, civilian safety, and decisions made under pressure. Each mission presents a different environment and set of objectives, asking players to assess the situation before committing to a course of action.

The game can be played solo with AI-controlled teammates or with friends in cooperative sessions. Its mission structure, detailed environments, and tense atmosphere make it a distinctive choice for players interested in realistic tactical gameplay.

Ready or Not also uses connected stories to explore crime and instability throughout Los Sueños. Briefings, environmental details, and mission objectives help build a wider picture of the city and the events unfolding within it.

## Setting and Story

### The City of Los Sueños

Los Sueños is a modern urban setting shaped by crime, public tension, and institutional challenges. Missions take place in varied locations, each with its own layout, atmosphere, and potential complications.

The setting gives each assignment a distinct identity. Players must pay attention to rooms, corridors, entrances, civilians, and other environmental details while working toward the mission objectives.

### Mission Driven Narrative

The story unfolds across connected assignments rather than through a single uninterrupted sequence of action. Briefings and discoveries help establish the circumstances behind each callout and the wider problems facing the city.

The narrative combines individual incidents with broader themes, giving context to the team's work and the decisions made during missions.

## Core Gameplay

### Tactical Mission Planning

Each assignment begins with a set of objectives and information about the situation. Players can review the mission briefing, consider the environment, and prepare for the challenges ahead.

Situational awareness is central to the experience. Rushing into an unfamiliar area can make it harder to identify threats, protect civilians, and complete objectives safely.

### Squad Coordination

Ready or Not places considerable emphasis on teamwork. Players can direct AI teammates during solo sessions or coordinate with human teammates during cooperative play.

Clear communication and shared awareness help the team maintain control of a changing situation. Team members must pay attention to the same objectives and react when new information becomes available.

### Civilian Safety and Rules of Engagement

Not every person encountered during a mission is a threat. Distinguishing between civilians and suspects is an important part of the game's decision-making system.

Mission outcomes reflect the choices made during an assignment. Players must balance completing objectives with protecting civilians and responding to situations within the game's rules of engagement.

### Environmental Awareness

Mission locations contain details that can affect how an assignment unfolds. Room layouts, visibility, available cover, and the placement of people all influence what players encounter.

Observing the environment and keeping track of the mission's changing circumstances are important elements of successful play.

### Solo and Cooperative Play

Ready or Not supports solo play with AI teammates and cooperative sessions with other players. Solo play lets players manage the team at their own pace, while cooperative sessions add communication and coordination between human participants.

Both modes center on mission objectives, situational awareness, and teamwork.

## Game Modes and Mission Variety

### Single Player

The single player mode lets players take command of a squad supported by AI teammates. It suits those who prefer a self-paced experience and want to focus on learning the mission layouts and objectives.

### Cooperative Sessions

Cooperative play allows friends to take part in missions together. Shared responsibility creates a different experience, as players need to communicate clearly, stay aware of teammates, and coordinate their decisions.

### Varied Mission Environments

Assignments use different locations and circumstances to maintain variety. Indoor environments, confined spaces, public areas, and changing mission conditions all influence the atmosphere and challenge of each assignment.

## Visual Design and Atmosphere

Ready or Not uses detailed indoor environments, subdued lighting, sound design, and environmental storytelling to create tension. The city feels unsettled, and each location communicates something about the circumstances surrounding the mission.

Audio cues can draw attention to activity beyond the player's immediate view, while visual details help establish the setting. These elements support a slower, more observant style of play than a conventional action shooter.
